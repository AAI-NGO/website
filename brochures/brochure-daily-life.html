Artificial Intelligence is transforming daily living across the globe. Our grassroots community session demystifies AI into an approachable, trustworthy tool for farmers, local shop owners, elders, and youth. Learn how AI can protect your harvest, solve daily problems, and inspire you to build enterprises that enrich village life.

🌾 Everyday Practical AI Modules

MODULE 01 • AGRI-TECH

AI for Farmers: Crops, Weather & Markets

Taking a smartphone photo of a diseased crop to identify pests instantly, receiving localized 7-day rainfall forecasts, and checking wholesale APMC market prices across Karnataka.

MODULE 02 • CITIZEN ADVISORY

Navigating Government Welfare Schemes

Asking AI in Kannada about eligibility for PM-KISAN, Raitha Siri, Gruha Lakshmi, Ayushman Bharat, and pension benefits, and generating step-by-step application checklists.

MODULE 03 • HEALTH & WELLNESS

Healthcare Awareness & Tele-Guidance

Translating doctor prescriptions into Kannada, understanding side-effects of common medicines, basic first-aid guidance, and nutrition planning for rural families.

MODULE 04 • SMALL COMMERCE

AI for Local Kirana, Traders & Services

How carpenters, electricians, plumbers, and kirana stores use AI for professional estimates, multilingual customer invoices, and digital business presence on Google Maps.

🌱 Fostering Grassroots Problem Solvers & Social Enterprises
We guide local youth to establish Village Innovation Kiosks — micro-enterprises providing digital assistance, soil-testing coordination, and crop advisory services to fellow villagers, creating local jobs while solving societal bottlenecks.
