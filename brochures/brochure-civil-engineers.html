The architecture, engineering, and construction (AEC) industry is undergoing a historic digital revolution. Civil engineers who master Building Information Modeling (BIM), open standards (IFC, BCF), and AI-driven quantity estimation will lead global infrastructure development. We train rural and urban engineers to design smart villages, manage complex projects, and launch high-margin civil-tech consulting firms.

📚 Specialized Curriculum & Training Modules

MODULE 01 • BIM REVOLUTION

Model-Centric Coordination with BIM & Open Standards

Mastering 3D/4D/5D BIM coordination using open standards (IFC, BCF, IDS). How multidisciplinary project teams eliminate site clashes, coordinate MEP/structural designs, and streamline execution.

MODULE 02 • ESTIMATION AI

Automated Quantity Take-off (QTO) & Cost Modeling

Using generative AI and computer vision to extract material quantities directly from architectural drawings and PDFs, reducing estimation time from days to under 30 minutes.

MODULE 03 • SMART VILLAGES

Digital Twins & Geospatial GIS for Rural Infrastructure

Applying GIS and Digital Twin modeling to rural road networks, decentralized water treatment, and watershed planning, building resilient, climate-proof villages.

MODULE 04 • ENTERPRISE INCUBATION

Launching Your Civil-Tech & BIM Consulting Firm

How young civil graduates can start independent BIM modeling bureaus, structural audit consultancies, and drone mapping agencies to serve Indian and international clients remotely.
