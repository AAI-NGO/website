A transformative, bilingual (Kannada & English) workshop designed for Universities, Degree Colleges, Pre-University (PUC), and High Schools. We bridge the metro-rural gap by giving students and teachers immediate mastery over artificial intelligence tools to learn faster, score higher, and secure high-demand careers.

📚 Workshop Curriculum & Key Training Modules

MODULE 01 • 20 MINS

AI as a 24/7 Personal Study Tutor

How students can turn ChatGPT and Gemini into private mentors to explain complex concepts in simple Kannada/English, solve mathematics doubts, and build spoken English fluency without costly coaching.

MODULE 02 • 20 MINS

Competitive Exam Acceleration

Using AI prompt engineering for KCET, NEET, Banking, and UPSC preparation: generating unlimited practice quizzes, memory mnemonics, and customized revision timetables.

MODULE 03 • 20 MINS

Future Careers & Building AI Enterprises

Introducing emerging careers (AI Prompting, Data Analysis, BIM & Civil Tech, Agri-Tech) and teaching youth how to build their own AI-enabled micro-enterprises to solve local challenges and innovate for society.

MODULE 04 • 15 MINS

Responsible AI & Digital Ethics

Critical digital hygiene: avoiding deepfakes, protecting personal data and Aadhaar privacy, verifying AI hallucinations, and upholding academic integrity.

🎓 Specialized Track for Teachers & Institutional Leaders
15-Minute Lesson Planning: Automate syllabus roadmaps, rubrics, and personalized remedial worksheets for slow learners.
Instant Question Bank Generator: Create balanced question papers based on Bloom's Taxonomy in minutes.
