Artificial Intelligence is not just for tech experts in skyscrapers. When placed in the hands of women in our villages, AI becomes a powerful catalyst for financial independence, household prosperity, and community leadership. We help women launch and scale their own AI-enabled local businesses to innovate and uplift society.

🌸 Practical, Hands-on Training Modules

MODULE 01 • VERNACULAR AI

Voice-First AI in Kannada & Hindi

Overcoming language and typing barriers: speaking directly to AI assistants in Kannada to get instant answers about children's education, health, agriculture, and government schemes.

MODULE 02 • DIGITAL COMMERCE

AI for Micro-Enterprises & Home Brands

How women can create product packaging labels, persuasive marketing descriptions, and catalog pictures for home-made foods, tailoring, handicrafts, and dairy products using free AI tools.

MODULE 03 • MARKET ACCESS

Direct Selling via WhatsApp & Digital Khata

Using AI-assisted business messaging to manage customer orders, track sales, calculate profit margins, and connect directly with urban buyers without exploitative middlemen.

MODULE 04 • FINANCIAL SECURITY

Financial Literacy & Cyber Fraud Safety

Safe digital payments, identifying online fraud and scam calls, securing bank accounts, and discovering interest-free micro-loans and government subsidies (Stree Shakti, PM Mudra).

💡 Transforming Women into Societal Innovators
Our mission is not merely digital literacy — it is entrepreneurial creation. We support Self-Help Group (SHG) members to build sustainable ventures, hire fellow village women, and create lasting economic value within their home communities.

✨ What Participants Gain

  • Confidence to interact with AI in native spoken Kannada
  • Step-by-step templates to launch a micro-business in 7 days
  • Verifiable AAI Certificate of Participation
  • Ongoing guidance from local Village AI Champions
  • Free entry into the AAI Rural Women Innovators Community
